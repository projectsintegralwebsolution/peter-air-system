About ArticAer

Engineering Air. Manufacturing Performance.

ArticAer is an engineering-driven HVAC manufacturing company delivering customized air-handling solutions for demanding applications across healthcare, pharmaceutical, industrial and commercial sectors.

We combine deep application understanding with in-house manufacturing capability — from initial engineering and selection to fabrication, quality inspection and project coordination.

Every ArticAer unit is configured to the specific requirements of the application: airflow, cooling load, filtration, static pressure, temperature, humidity and spatial constraints. Our manufacturing facility enables full control over quality, lead times and customization — ensuring that every project receives equipment built precisely to specification.
